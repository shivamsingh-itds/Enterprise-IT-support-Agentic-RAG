# ShivTech Internal IT Support Handbook

## VPN Access
Employees working outside the office must use the ShivTech VPN before accessing internal systems. Install the ShivTech Secure VPN client from the internal Software Portal. Sign in with your company email and password, complete MFA, and select the closest corporate gateway. If the connection fails, first confirm internet connectivity, restart the client, and try another corporate gateway. Do not disable endpoint security or firewall settings to make the VPN work. If the problem remains, open an IT ticket with a screenshot of the error.

## Password Reset Policy
Company passwords must be at least 14 characters. Employees should use the self-service identity portal for normal password resets. After five failed login attempts, the account may be temporarily locked. If self-service recovery fails, contact the IT Service Desk. Employees must never share passwords or MFA codes with support personnel.

## Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
All employees must enable MFA for their ShivTech accounts. The approved authentication methods are the company authenticator application and registered security keys. If you get a new phone, register the authenticator app on your new device through the identity portal. Never approve an MFA request that you did not initiate. Report unexpected authentication requests to IT immediately.

## Software Installation
Employees must install software only from the official ShivTech Software Portal. Administrative permissions are required for installing certain applications. If an application is missing from the portal, submit a software installation request with the application name, business purpose, and manager approval. Do not download cracked, pirated, or unauthorized software.

## Email and Outlook Support
Employees should access company email through the approved Outlook application or web portal. If emails are not syncing, check the internet connection, restart Outlook, and verify that the correct account is configured. If the issue continues, clear the Outlook cache only after following IT-approved instructions. Report suspicious emails using the Report Phishing option.

## Microsoft Teams Support
If Microsoft Teams is not connecting, check your internet connection and confirm that you are signed in with your ShivTech account. Restart Teams and check whether the application has pending updates. If audio or video is not working, verify the selected microphone, camera, and speaker devices in Teams settings. Contact IT if the issue persists.

## Wi-Fi and Network Connectivity
Employees working from the office should connect to the official ShivTech corporate Wi-Fi network. Use your company credentials when prompted. If Wi-Fi is not working, restart the device, check whether airplane mode is disabled, and reconnect to the network. Do not connect company devices to unknown or unsecured public networks. Report repeated connectivity issues to the IT Service Desk.

## Laptop and Hardware Support
Employees must keep company laptops, chargers, monitors, keyboards, and other accessories in good working condition. If a laptop becomes slow, restart the device and close unnecessary applications. For hardware damage, battery issues, overheating, or display problems, raise an IT support ticket. Employees must not open or repair company hardware themselves.

## Printer and Scanner Support
Employees should use the printers configured on the ShivTech office network. If a printer is not responding, check its power connection, paper supply, and network connection. Confirm that the correct printer is selected on your computer. If the issue continues, restart the print queue or contact IT support. Report toner and cartridge replacement requirements through an IT ticket.

## Data Security and Access Control
Employees must access only the files, applications, and systems required for their job responsibilities. Do not share confidential company information through personal email accounts or unauthorized file-sharing platforms. Lock your computer whenever you leave your workstation. Report suspected unauthorized access or data exposure to the IT Security Team immediately.

## Employee Account Access
New employees receive system access based on their assigned role and department. Managers must submit access requests for new team members through the IT Service Desk. When employees change departments or responsibilities, their access permissions must be reviewed and updated. Employees must not use another person's account to access company systems.

## Browser and Internet Issues
If a website is not loading, first verify your internet connection and try refreshing the page. Clear the browser cache and cookies if the issue continues. Use company-approved browsers and keep them updated. If internal company portals remain inaccessible, report the affected website URL and any error message to IT support.

## Data Backup and File Recovery
Employees should save important work files to approved ShivTech storage locations. Local desktop or download folders should not be treated as permanent storage. If a file is accidentally deleted or modified, contact IT support as soon as possible with the file name, original location, and approximate deletion time. Do not attempt recovery using unauthorized software.

## IT Support Ticket Process
Employees should create an IT support ticket for technical issues that cannot be resolved using standard troubleshooting steps. Include the issue description, affected device, application name, error message, and screenshots when available. Select the appropriate issue category and priority. Do not include passwords, MFA codes, or other sensitive credentials in ticket descriptions.

## Remote Work Security
Employees working remotely must use the ShivTech VPN when accessing internal resources. Company devices must have approved antivirus and endpoint protection enabled. Avoid using public computers for company work. Do not leave company devices unattended in public places. Report lost or stolen company devices to IT and your manager immediately.

## Incident Reporting
Employees must immediately report suspected malware infections, phishing attacks, lost devices, unauthorized access, or accidental data sharing. Do not attempt to investigate or remove suspected malware independently. Disconnect the affected device from the network if instructed by IT or the security team. Provide relevant details through the approved incident reporting process.
